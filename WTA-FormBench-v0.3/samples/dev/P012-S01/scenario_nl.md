An air-defense command system must assign radars and missile vehicles to intercept incoming aerial targets. For each target, one or more interception attempts may be planned. A valid scheme must coordinate radar tracking, missile vehicle assignment and interception timing while respecting radar channel limits, missile load limits, detection feasibility and engagement feasibility. The objective depends on the firepower situation: when defensive resources are sufficient, cost-effective interception matters; when attacks are saturated, interception value dominates.
