Build an air-defense deployment optimization model. The upper-level decision selects suitable candidate positions for a fixed number of air-defense systems. The enemy drone swarm chooses a minimum-cost path through the region, where path cost depends on distance, altitude, turns, slope and fire-threat exposure. After an interception, the drone swarm replans from a new starting point while avoiding or accounting for already deployed systems. The defender maximizes the total induced path/replanning cost.
