Build a target-launcher-time WTA model. A binary decision indicates whether launcher w engages target t at time slot s. The kill probability for an engagement is computed from relative distance and heading error. A launcher can engage at most one target at a time, each target has a maximum number of assigned interceptors, each launcher has limited usable interceptors, and assignments are allowed only in feasible firing time slots. Consecutive firings from the same launcher must respect a firing delay.
