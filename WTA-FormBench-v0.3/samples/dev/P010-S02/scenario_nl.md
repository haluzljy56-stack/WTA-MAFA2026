Build a weapon-target assignment model with binary weapon-target decisions and binary target-destruction decisions. Each weapon can be assigned to at most one target, while a target may receive multiple weapons. The kill effect of several assigned weapons against the same target is combined through independent failure terms. A target may be declared destroyed only when its combined kill probability reaches its target-specific threshold. The objective rewards destroyed target value and penalizes overkill and invalid assignments to undestroyed targets.
