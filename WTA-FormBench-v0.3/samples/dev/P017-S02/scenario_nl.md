Build a time-sampling dynamic WTA model. At each sampling time, each weapon chooses one missile number from the current missile storage list or chooses no attack. A missile is selectable only if it is currently available in the storage list. The threat degree of a missile is its damage intensity divided by its distance to the protected asset. The current-step objective rewards assigning weapons to high-threat missiles with high destruction probabilities. The missile storage list is updated between sampling times as missiles are destroyed, hit the asset, disappear, or newly enter the list.
