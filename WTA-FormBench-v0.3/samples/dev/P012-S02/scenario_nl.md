Build an air-defense resource assignment model. For each target and possible interception attempt, select at most one missile vehicle, at most one radar and a feasible interception time. A planned interception must have both radar tracking and missile vehicle assignment. Radar tracking must begin early enough, radars have limited channels over time, and missile vehicles have limited loads. Consecutive interceptions of the same target must be separated by an allowed time interval. Interception probability depends on radar accuracy and missile engagement feasibility.
