Build the WTA model and its continuous relaxation. In the original model, each weapon selects one target, and target survival is the product of the failure probabilities of all weapons assigned to that target. The objective is to minimize the value-weighted expected surviving target value. In the relaxed model, binary decisions are replaced by continuous effort variables in [0,1], and the convex relaxed objective uses powers of the failure probabilities. Per-weapon effort must not exceed one.
