Build a joint task-assignment and bandwidth-allocation model for cooperative suppressive jamming. The binary task decision assigns each UAV to at most one radar or to no task. The continuous bandwidth decision specifies how much bandwidth each assigned UAV contributes to the radar it suppresses. For each suppressed radar, the assigned UAVs' bandwidths must sum to that radar's working bandwidth. The reward increases with the bandwidth-aware jamming effect and saturates, while the cost increases with the number of participating UAVs.
