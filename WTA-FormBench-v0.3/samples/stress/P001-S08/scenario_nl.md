Use binary assignment decisions for sensing-to-target and effector-to-target choices. For each target, combine the failure probabilities of the assigned sensing assets to obtain target detection confidence, combine the failure probabilities of the assigned effectors to obtain conditional destruction confidence, and require the product of those two confidences to be at least the common threshold. Each sensing asset and each effector is assigned to no more than one target, and each target receives at least one of each.
