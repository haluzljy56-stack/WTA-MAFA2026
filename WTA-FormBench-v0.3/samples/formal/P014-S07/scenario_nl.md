Formulate the static three-objective SWTA model with binary sensor-target and weapon-target assignment decisions. Compute each target's tracking probability as one minus the product of sensor failure probabilities over assigned sensors, and its destruction probability as one minus the product of weapon failure probabilities over assigned weapons. Minimize the negative normalized value-weighted product of tracking and destruction effects, minimize total activation/launch cost of assigned sensors and weapons, and minimize interceptor use relative to the total ammunition baseline. Enforce at least one sensor and at least two weapons per target, per-resource capacity limits, and binary domains. This controlled variant changes only the declared resource or candidate restriction field from the base public data.
