Formulate the WTAU model. Use binary decisions for weapon-to-target assignment and binary decisions indicating whether each target is destroyed. For each target, compute survival as the product of the failure probabilities of all weapons assigned to it, and compute kill probability as one minus that survival. Maximize destroyed target value, subtract an overkill penalty for destroyed targets whose achieved kill probability exceeds the threshold, and subtract an invalid-kill penalty for weapons assigned to targets not declared destroyed. Enforce that each weapon is assigned to at most one target and that a target can be declared destroyed only if its required kill threshold is reached.
