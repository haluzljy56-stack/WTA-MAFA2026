Use binary assignment decisions only for eligible missile-target pairs. A missile is eligible when it is healthy and its target is within range. For a missile assigned inside an area, its survival against interception is calculated from the enemy interceptor count in that area and the number of defender missiles allocated to targets in that same area. Multiply that survival value by the missile's intrinsic reliability, electromagnetic-environment reliability, and target-specific damage probability. Combine assigned missiles as independent attempts for each target, combine targets in an area as a serial mission, and maximize the two objectives: value-weighted efficiency per cost and overall mission reliability.
