Formulate the AMTA model with binary missile-target assignment decisions. Each missile is assigned to exactly one target, while a target may receive multiple missiles. Compute missile-target kill probability from missile payload and target health. For an assigned target, combine the kill effects of missiles that penetrate the adversarial defense through independent failure terms. Maximize expected target-value-weighted combat effectiveness over the stochastic penetration outcomes. When the multiobjective variant is activated, also minimize the total cost of launched/assigned missiles as a separate objective rather than as a hidden weighted penalty.
